03 / The fine print matters

Incentives, with
eyes open.

The playbook outlines incentives and key cross-border tax and trade considerations. Eligibility depends on the relevant criteria.

INCENTIVES / OVERVIEW

Know what is listed.

  • The overview lists 5% corporate tax for 10 or 15 years for qualifying manufacturing activities, alongside capital investment thresholds of RM500 million and RM1 billion, excluding land.
  • 5% personal income tax for knowledge workers.
  • Sector-specific incentives, grants and facilitation support are also covered.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, printed p. 3 (PDF page 6)

TAX & TRADE / WATCH-OUTS

Plan across borders.

  • Permanent establishment: where work is performed and decisions are made can affect tax exposure.
  • Transfer pricing: related-party transactions should follow the arm’s length principle and be documented.
  • Employees: track Johor workdays; the JS-SEZ does not override domestic tax rules or immigration obligations.
  • Customs: maintain accurate origin, HS code and supporting trade data.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, printed pp. 14–17 (PDF pp. 17–20)

! Incentive terms and tax obligations depend on your activities and eligibility. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authorities before making decisions.

04 / People & mobility

Plan the work.
Then the pass.

The appendix describes several pass routes. The right option depends on the person’s role, activities and time in Malaysia.

SMF JS-SEZ Playbook, Appendix, printed pp. 28–29 (PDF pp. 31–32)

EP

Employment Pass

Appendix salary bands: Category I, RM10,000 or more; Category II, RM5,000–RM9,999; Category III, RM3,000–RM4,999. The appendix lists 1 June 2026 as the effective date for revised thresholds.

Applications and approvals are subject to criteria in the appendix.
PVP

Professional Visit Pass

For specified temporary professional work, such as training, consultancy or project-based work. The appendix says it can last up to 12 months and is not extendable.

IP

Investor Pass for business travellers

For business travellers in the new investor, investor in the pipeline or existing investor categories. The appendix describes stays of up to 12 months, in periods of up to six months at a time, subject to requirements.

! Pass details are summarised from the playbook appendix. Check current rules and application requirements before travel or hiring.
